## Data used by the paper
Telegram pump channels and their announced pumps on Binance and other exchanges, with CoinGecko / exchange statistics of
every candidate coin before the scheduled time; per channel the ordered list of its previously pumped coins.

## Split and threshold conventions in the paper
Chronological train / validation / test split over pump events; for each event all candidate coins are ranked one hour
before the scheduled pump; metrics are ranking metrics (HR@k) and AUC. No per-bar alarm threshold.

## What was adapted and why
* The benchmark has no announced schedule and one episode per column, so a row is (bar t, episode i) and the model
  outputs a per-row pump probability.
* Channel: the social source id with the largest prior involvement (`mark[3]`) among the social events usable at t.
  The loader's `channel` field is NOT read, because for pump episodes it is only knowable at the announcement.
* Sequence: train pump episodes in which that source appeared and whose window ended before the scored episode's window
  starts (strictly realised history), most recent first; element = that episode's causal market features at the bar
  before its label onset (labels read through `labels_for(train_assets)` inside `_fit` only) + hashed coin embedding.
* Candidate features: causal market features over {3, 12, 48} bars instead of CoinGecko statistics.
* Without a known social source the sequence / channel inputs are zero and the model is the official DNN on the
  candidate features ("market branch only").
* Rows with no defined market feature yet abstain (NaN).

## Unspecified choices and how they were fixed
1. A source is a known organiser when `mark[3] >= 1` (`model.min_prior`) or it appears in >= 2 train pumps
   (`model.min_history`); ties: larger prior, longer train history, first seen. It does not expire within the episode.
2. History cut-off by `meta['bar_close_ns']` (window end of the earlier episode < window start of the scored one);
   fallback `anchor_ns`, then column order.
3. Hash buckets: 256 coins, 64 channels, embedding size 8; max_seq 50, heads 8 as official.
4. Class imbalance: pos_weight = n_neg / n_pos capped at 50; negatives sub-sampled to 60 000 rows; 10 epochs; weight
   decay 1e-5.
5. Smoke mode switches the coin embedding off (synthetic symbols are unique per episode).
